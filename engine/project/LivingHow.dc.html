HONEST ABOUT IT
Mapped hereFootprints and years (98%) from the BAG import, heights (89%) from 3D BAG, the Westerkerk and its tower in parts, canals, 39 houseboats, 285 street surfaces, 243 lit streets and 132 opening hours.
Guessed, and shown as guessedColour (0.1% mapped), floors (2%), roof shape (2%) and tree size (none). Guesses are only ever values of those real tags. What is mapped turns each one white.
Places, by kind and by brandEvery shop, café and hotel gets its category’s colour and glyph; chains carry their mapped brand=*, in colours from Atlas’s own style table, never a logo. Independents go by kind. Terraces only where outdoor_seating says so.
Not drawn, because it is not a tagNo cars, no people, no lit windows, no moored boats beyond the 39 mapped. None of it is OpenStreetMap data, so none of it is on this map.
Weather, as it was or as forecastOpen-Meteo’s hourly model: Wednesday as it happened, Thursday as forecast. Water level and quay walls are drawn generic, as no tag gives them here; every reflection mirrors only mapped things.
On the phoneTiles, sun, shadows and light are worked out on the device, so it works offline and nothing about where you look leaves the phone.
